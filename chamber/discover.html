About Ibadan

Ibadan is the capital of Oyo State and the third-largest city in Nigeria by population. Known for its rich Yoruba heritage, it is home to the University of Ibadan — Nigeria's oldest university — Cocoa House (West Africa's first skyscraper), and the historic Mapo Hall. The city is a major commercial and agricultural hub, driving trade across the South-West.
